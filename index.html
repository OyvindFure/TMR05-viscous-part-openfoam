Viscous flow, part 2: overview

The whole OpenFOAM part in one place: what the assignment asks for, what we have run, and what is left. One page per study goes deeper.

What is the assignment?

A box (B = 6 m, B/D = 4) is forced to oscillate up and down in water at one frequency (ω̂ = 1.0). In part 1, WAMIT computed the force and the waves for a frictionless fluid. In part 2 we do the same in OpenFOAM with the real fluid (viscosity and a free surface), step by step in time. Then we compare: added mass, damping and radiated waves.

Because the simulation is numerical, we must show that the answer does not depend on the chosen settings. That is what the sensitivity studies do: the same case, four values of one setting at a time.

What is left

  • Frequency: the assignment asks for coefficients at different frequencies. We only have ω̂ = 1.0.
  • Phase: differs by about 180 degrees from WAMIT. A convention that is not resolved. Do not use the phase in the report until it is.
  • Figures and text: figures for domain, time step and ramp-up, and the chapters themselves.
  • Optional: a ParaView image of the initial condition.

Not assigned to us, but in the assignment

Time marching scheme (4 combinations of time schemes and time steps) and turbulence modelling (laminar against two models with different parameters). Only the groups of five have time marching. Mention that they exist, or run them if you want the full picture.
